Comments & Observations
Soil organic matter levels remain within normal parameters for the Dark Brown soil zone. Slight compaction noted in the 15-30 cm subsoil horizon; deep tillage or cover cropping with taproot species is recommended to relieve subsoil density. Salinity levels are currently low with minimal crop impact anticipated for standard cereal and pulse rotations.
* Crop impact description: Based on standard provincial yield reduction estimates relative to electrical conductivity readings for non-resistant crops.
** WEOM: Water Extractable Organic Matter, representing the biologically active fraction of soil organic matter.

Summary of Methods

pH and EC measured via 1:1 soil-water suspension. Total organic carbon and total nitrogen determined by dry combustion (LECO). Texture determined by hydrometer method. WEOM extracted using ambient temperature deionized water extraction followed by TOC analysis.
